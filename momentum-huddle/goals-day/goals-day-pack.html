FY27 goals day

The rules of the day

  1. One idea per card. Marker, one sentence, something a stranger could act on Monday.
  2. Mark the source in the top corner: ★ Stuart · T team backlog · N new today. The letter counts, not the colour of the paper.
  3. No number without a source next to it. Don’t know it? Write “TBD · where it lives · who checks by Monday”. Never guess.
  4. Silent when the clock says write. Talk when it says stick, claim, review or commit.
  5. The clock is the boss. One +1 minute per phase, by a show of hands. The clock-keeper changes every phase so the facilitator plays too.
  6. Claim with a flag (your initials). 2 to 4 business goals each, plus 1 personal. Two flags on one card: 60 seconds, one owner; the other is written on the sheet as “depends on”.
  7. Every ★ card leaves the wall with a flag or a tag: OWNED · STUART’S OWN · OTHER TEAM · FOLDED INTO #__. No orphans.
  8. Review the goal, not the person. Four checks, one improvement, one verdict: ✔ Ready · ✎ Fix one thing · ↺ Rethink. The author answers every improvement in writing: “Taken” or “No, because …”.
  9. A goal is committed when the sheet is complete and signed: area, shift, metric, source, baseline, target, date, done-when, Q1 milestone by 31 Oct, Q2 milestone by 31 Jan.
  10. Off-topic goes to the Parking lot header. Nothing is lost, nothing derails.
The four checks (every reviewer, every goal)
  • Specific enough that a stranger could start Monday?
  • Has a number, a source and a date?
  • Names the FY27 goal it serves — and would Stuart agree it does?
  • Could the owner hit this metric without helping the business? (If yes, fix the metric.)
Brief: 3 to 5 goals each, one of them personal. FY27 runs 1 Aug 2026 – 31 Jul 2027; Q1 ends 31 Oct, Q2 ends 31 Jan.

Facilitator · last 20 minutes

Lock checklist

  • Coverage. Walk the wall. Every ★ card has a signed sheet on it or a tag (STUART’S OWN · OTHER TEAM · FOLDED INTO #). Read the untagged ones aloud and tag them now.
  • Load. Count sheets per person: 3 to 5 including the personal one. Under 3: claim from the wall now. Over 5: fold or release one.
  • Dependencies. Anyone whose sheet says “depends on” another person or another team says so aloud; the other person initials it or it goes to the Parking lot with a name.
  • Dates. Every business sheet has a Q1 milestone dated on or before 31 Oct 2026 and a Q2 milestone on or before 31 Jan 2027.
  • Rate the day. Everyone writes 1–10 on a card, face down, then all up at once. Anything under 8: “where did we lose you?” in one sentence, to the Parking lot.
  • Photos. The whole wall, each area close enough to read, and every signed sheet, front and back.
  • Typist. Name who types the sheets into the workbook, and by when (Fri 25 Sep). Write it on the Parking lot header.

What happens after

Each signed sheet is typed into the owner’s “Name — FY27 Goals” tab in the team workbook, one goal block per sheet, exactly as written on paper. The block already exists in the tab:

Then every Q1 milestone becomes one priority on the weekly huddle’s Priorities page, owner = the signer, due 31 Oct 2026, so the goals are reviewed every week from the first huddle after the day. Tags on unclaimed ★ cards and everything on the Parking lot go under the personal goals as a short list. Nothing from the day becomes a to-do.
