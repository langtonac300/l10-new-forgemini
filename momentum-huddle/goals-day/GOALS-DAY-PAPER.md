# FY27 goals day on paper — rules, run of show, what happens after

**Thu 2026-09-24 · 4 hours · a rented conference room at Expansive, Cathedral Square · five
people: Alex, Courtney, Scott, CJ, Allaina.** The Forge app is parked for this day (see
[`IDEA-FORGE.md`](../IDEA-FORGE.md)); the day runs on a wall, sticky notes, markers and
printed sheets. The shape is the one the app settled on in v2.18, because it was designed
against the same brief: **seeded wall → one round of new ideas → claim → write the goal →
one peer review → commit → lock.**

The printable pack is [`goals-day-pack.html`](./goals-day-pack.html) (20 pages, letter).
It is **generated** by [`build-pack.js`](./build-pack.js) from the `FORGE_LINES` and
`FORGE_SEED_CARDS` defaults in `apps-script/L10Setup.gs`, so the 12 goal areas and the 39
cards on paper are exactly the app's deck. Edit the deck there, run `node build-pack.js`,
reprint. The pack carries only neutral wording (it goes on a wall the whole team reads).

## The rules (the poster)

1. **One idea per card.** Marker, one sentence, something a stranger could act on Monday.
2. **Mark the source in the top corner:** ★ Stuart · T team backlog · N new today. *The letter
   counts, not the colour of the paper* (Alex is colourblind; colour never carries meaning alone).
3. **No number without a source next to it.** Unknown? Write "TBD · where it lives · who checks
   by Monday". Never guess. (The app's "never invent a number" rule, on paper.)
4. **Silent when the clock says write.** Talk when it says stick, claim, review or commit.
5. **The clock is the boss.** One +1 minute per phase, by a show of hands. The **clock-keeper
   rotates every phase** so the facilitator (Alex) writes and claims like everyone else.
6. **Claim with a flag** (your initials on a page-marker flag or sticker). 2 to 4 business goals
   each, plus 1 personal. Two flags on one card: 60 seconds, one owner; the other is written on the
   sheet as "depends on".
7. **Every ★ card leaves the wall with a flag or a tag:** OWNED · STUART'S OWN · OTHER TEAM ·
   FOLDED INTO #__. No orphans — this is how the team proves it read the boss's list.
8. **Review the goal, not the person.** Four checks, one improvement, one verdict
   (✔ Ready · ✎ Fix one thing · ↺ Rethink). The author answers every improvement in writing:
   "Taken" or "No, because …".
9. **A goal is committed when the sheet is complete and signed:** area, shift, metric, source,
   baseline, target, date, done-when, Q1 milestone by 31 Oct, Q2 milestone by 31 Jan.
10. **Off-topic goes to the Parking lot header.** Nothing is lost, nothing derails.

**The four checks** (the app's doctor checklist, unchanged):
specific enough that a stranger could start Monday? · has a number, a source and a date? ·
names the FY27 goal it serves, and would Stuart agree it does? · could the owner hit this metric
*without* helping the business? (if yes, fix the metric).

## Run of show (240 minutes)

| Clock | Min | Phase | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | 15 | Doors | Wall is up before anyone arrives: 12 area headers left to right, 39 cards under them, "New today" and "Parking lot" at the far right, rules poster on the wall. |
| 0:15 | 15 | Brief | Stuart's 12 areas read off the wall (5) · the rules (5) · the four checks and a blank goal sheet (5). |
| 0:30 | 20 | New ideas | **8 min silent:** what is missing for *your* accounts? Add an idea, make one of Stuart's concrete, or name something to stop. One idea per card, N in the corner. **12 min stick-and-say:** round the table, one card at a time, read it in ten seconds, stick it under an area or under New today. Duplicates are stacked, never binned. |
| 0:50 | 15 | Claim | **8 min gallery walk:** flag the cards you will turn into goals (2–4 flags). **7 min:** contested cards get 60 seconds each; every ★ card without a flag gets a tag. Count flags per person aloud. |
| 1:05 | 10 | Break | |
| 1:15 | 45 | Write the goal | Silent, at the table. Flagged cards come down; one business goal sheet per card (≈10 min each), then the personal sheet. |
| 2:00 | 25 | Peer review | **12 min:** pass your sheets left; the reviewer ticks the four checks, writes one improvement and a verdict on each sheet. **3 min:** hand back. **10 min:** author writes "Taken" / "No, because …" and fixes the sheet. |
| 2:25 | 10 | Break | |
| 2:35 | 50 | Commit | Stand at the wall. Each business goal read in 30 seconds; the room may ask only "Would Stuart agree this serves that area?" and "What is done by 31 Oct?". The owner writes the Q1 and Q2 milestones, signs, sticks the sheet over its card. Personal goals: one sentence each, no questions. Up to 20 business goals (5 people × max 4) at about 2 min each. |
| 3:25 | 20 | Lock | Coverage of every ★ card · 3–5 sheets per person · dependencies said aloud and initialled · dates checked · rate the day 1–10 on a card, face down then all up · photos of the wall and every sheet · **name the typist and the date**. |
| 3:45 | 15 | Buffer | Pack up. Sheets leave with their owners; the photos are the record until typed. |

If Write runs long, take the time from Commit (20-second read-outs), never from Peer review.

## Why these mechanics

- **Seeded wall + one ideas round** — the goals are meant to come from Stuart's list; the round
  exists to fill gaps and to surface "stop doing" items, not to generate forty ideas.
- **Flags, not dot-voting** — with five people and 3–5 goals each, the scarce thing is ownership,
  not popularity. A flag is a claim; a dot is an opinion.
- **The ★ coverage rule** — the failure mode of a team goals day is skipping the boss's list. Every
  Stuart card leaving with a flag or a written tag makes that impossible to do silently.
- **Two questions at Commit** — keeps 20 read-outs inside 50 minutes and keeps the room on the two
  things a reviewer cannot judge from paper: relevance to Stuart and the first milestone.
- **Sheet mirrors the tab** — typing is a copy, not a translation; nothing gets reinterpreted after
  the room empties.
- **Static roles** would leave the facilitator out of their own goals; a rotating clock-keeper
  gives every phase an owner and lets Alex play.
